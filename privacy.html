Privacy Policy

How Evential handles data across the Event Dashboard and Sponsor Discovery. Last updated: 5/31/2026.

1. Who we are

Evential, LLC ("Evential", "we", "us") provides smart-badge hardware, event analytics and sponsor discovery tools for event organizers. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. Questions: contact@evential.co.

2. What we collect

Event Dashboard. During an event, smart badges and gateway sensors capture movement signals, check-ins, booth visits, dwell time and session attendance. Movement data is anonymous by default: we track badge identifiers, not personal identities. A badge is only linked to a person's contact details when that attendee explicitly opts in to become a lead for a sponsor.

Sponsor Discovery. Company sponsorship information is compiled from public sources (event websites, sponsor pages, press releases and company sites). When you create an account or export data, we collect your name, email and billing details.

3. How we use it

To generate event analytics and sponsor ROI reports, to provide sponsor rankings and contact routes, to operate and improve the service, to process payments, and to communicate with you. We do not sell personal data.

4. Sharing

Opt-in lead data is shared with the specific event sponsor the attendee chose to engage with. We use service providers (hosting, payments, analytics) under contract. We may disclose data where required by law.

5. Your rights

Depending on your location you may request access, correction, deletion or portability of your personal data, and may object to certain processing. Event organizers using the dashboard are provided with privacy notice and consent templates that meet applicable regional requirements. To exercise rights, email contact@evential.co.

6. Retention & security

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards.

7. Changes

We'll update this policy as the product evolves and post the revised date above.
