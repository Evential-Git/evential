Event analytics

Know what's happening on your floor, while you can still fix it.

The same badge data that proves sponsor ROI shows you how the event is actually going. See the bottlenecks, the dead zones and the packed rooms in real time, then act before attendees ever complain.

Session engagement

Real attendance, fill rate and drop-off for every session, what lands and what empties the room.

In practice: you notice the 2pm workshop is half full while the overflow room next door is packed, and merge them at the break.

Crowd behavior & flow

How people move between rooms, where they cluster, and which paths jam up between sessions.

In practice: the hallway by registration bottlenecks at 9am, so next day you open a second entrance.

Live floor heatmap

A real gradient heatmap of your venue built from badge density, so brighter areas mean more visitors.

In practice: a speaker asks for a live show of hands and sees the result on stage in seconds.

Safety & compliance

Live capacity and overflow alerts, plus built-in privacy compliance templates. Movement data is anonymous by default.

In practice: the main hall hits 96% and you get pinged to open the overflow room before the fire code is an issue.

Recommended optimizations

Concrete fixes ranked by impact, where to move a session, reroute traffic, or relocate a quiet booth.

In practice: the dashboard suggests moving a popular talk to the big room tomorrow, and you do.

Automations

Trigger waitlist releases, overflow routing, sponsor alerts and report delivery automatically when activity crosses a threshold.

In practice: when a session frees up 8 seats from no-shows, the waitlist is notified without anyone lifting a finger.

Better events make sponsors easier to keep.

Engagement data doubles as renewal evidence. When you can show a sponsor their booth got 2× the traffic because you rerouted the floor toward them, next year sells itself.
