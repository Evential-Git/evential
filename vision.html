Evential · Ten-year vision

The measurement layer for real‑world marketing.

Digital marketing has clicks, impressions and attribution. Physical events still rely on scans, anecdotes and post-show surveys.

We believe the physical world deserves a measurement layer of its own.

What exists nowBooth-level traffic, dwell, repeat visits and exhibitor performance reports.What this page describesOur intended direction. This is not a claim that every future capability exists today.
The premise

Attention moves through rooms. Investment should be able to follow it.

Every conference, trade show, campus event and community gathering is a live expression of what people care about. Which ideas fill a room? Which exhibitors earn a return visit? Which communities attract attention but not funding?

Today, most of that signal disappears when the doors close. Our ten-year aim is to make aggregate physical engagement legible, first for the people running the event, then for the organizations deciding where to invest next.

Three layers

Capture. Prove. Scale.

Not three separate products. Three stages of the same system becoming more useful over time.

01

Capture the physical signal.

Make presence and movement measurable without asking attendees to live inside another app.

  • Current: badge and gateway deployments
  • Future direction: batteryless “Pixels”
  • Future direction: precise venue-scale positioning
02

Prove what earned engagement.

Turn raw presence into a result organizers and exhibitors can inspect and compare.

  • Published measurement rules
  • Organizer investigation prompts
  • Performance reports and permitted contacts
03

Scale better investment decisions.

Connect the event, company, contact, category and outcome so the next sponsorship starts better informed.

  • Event-specific sponsor fit
  • Evidence-ranked contact routes
  • Aggregate interest and funding patterns

The path

Build the truth before building the network.

  1. Now

    Make floor measurement reliable.

    Deploy repeatably, publish the rule, deliver a useful report, and earn organizer trust one event at a time.

  2. Next

    Make live events more responsive.

    Turn measurement into better placement, programming, capacity and follow-up decisions while the event can still improve.

  3. Then

    Connect discovery to demonstrated outcomes.

    Rank likely sponsors and contacts using event fit, geography, history and measured performance, not a purchased title list alone.

  4. Ten-year aim

    Make real-world marketing measurable and repeatable.

    Help organizers, sponsors and communities understand where attention forms and where the next dollar can do more.

The guardrails

What we refuse to become matters as much as what we build.

Not individual surveillance.

Identity linkage requires consent. Community intelligence must be aggregated and designed to prevent re-identification.

Not invented attribution.

Presence, dwell and return visits are observable. Intent, conversation quality and monetary value are not inferred from a badge.

Not a black-box score.

Important measures need a published basis, visible limitations and a dispute path.

Not data for its own sake.

If a metric does not improve an organizer, exhibitor or community decision, it should not exist.

Different layers. One finish line: make sponsorship measurable, repeatable and worth more.
