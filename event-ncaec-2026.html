What it let them do

Four findings, four decisions.

Price the floor on evidenceThe busiest monitored booth scored 2.1× the event median, a difference wide enough to price differently.
Design for circulationFifty-four qualified leads reached two or more sponsors. The strongest paths show which adjacencies create shared value.
Place booths on evidenceCorner booths averaged 78 visitors and edge booths 67. The floor can now be designed around observed traffic.
Renew with a shared floor storyEvery sponsor sees its own result while NCAEC can show how attention circulated across the whole event.

One booth that had a quiet show

Not every booth performs the same, and the report says so.

One of the ten monitored booths converted 32% of recorded visitors into QVS-1 qualified visits, below the 41% event average, with dwell time under half the norm. Its report presents that result in the same format as every other booth's.

A quiet booth is a signal to investigate placement, staffing, activation and offer before the next event. The measurement identifies the difference; it does not claim which factor caused it.
