The first Evential dashboard is live.

From manually exported prototype data to a platform for organizers and sponsors: a look back at the April 5 release.

On April 5, we shipped the first iteration of the Evential dashboard. It marked a shift from manually exporting prototype data to giving organizers and sponsors a platform where they could explore their event.

An overview of the event floor

The release brought check-ins, foot traffic, dwell time, and booth visits into a live overview, with support for multiple events. The goal was to make activity on the floor easier to understand while the event was still unfolding.

A clearer view for sponsors

The April update introduced a sponsor report marketplace and a report experience covering leads, engagement scores, dwell time, and repeat visits, with CSV and PDF exports. Organizers could offer qualified reports, with notifications supporting the process.

Next on the roadmap: automations

Automations were still coming soon in this update. The planned workflows would connect live event triggers to actions, including webhooks and tools such as Zapier and Make.
