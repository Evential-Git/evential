How to use it

From your event details to a sponsor list in minutes.

Tell us about your event

Enter your event category, city and expected attendees. No account needed to start searching.

Free, no signup to browse

Get a ranked shortlist

Evential scores every tracked company on category, location and size fit, and surfaces the strongest matches first. Each row shows why they fit — the signal behind the score.

Filter to your reality

Narrow by industry, event type, location, company size or sponsor tier until the list matches the sponsors you can realistically land.

Open a profile

Dig into sponsorship history, typical event size, common co-sponsors and the contact routes for their partnership, events and field-marketing teams.

Draft your outreach

Use the built-in email assist to turn a profile into a first-draft pitch you can edit — so you're not staring at a blank page.

Export when you're ready

Export your shortlist with full data columns to run outreach in your own tools. Browsing is free; export is billed per row.
